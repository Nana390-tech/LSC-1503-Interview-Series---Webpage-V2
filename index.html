Know before you go

Assessment snapshot

Treat the assessment like a real professional interview from the moment you arrive.

20%

Assessment weight

This interview contributes 20% of the course grade.

Week 8

Assessment week

Your teacher will confirm the scheduled date and time.

3–5

Minutes

Every answer matters, so stay focused and give clear examples.

In person

Interview format

An individual, face-to-face interview that is audio recorded.

Q1
MANDATORYTell me about yourself.
Q2
MANDATORYWhich study skill might be most useful in your future job? Give an example.

Your teacher will also select questions about self-description, past experiences, or future plans and may ask follow-up questions.

Your success guide

How marks are earned

Depth wins marks: say it, show it with a true example, then explain why it matters.

40%

Content

  • Answer the question you hear
  • Give true, detailed examples
  • Listen actively
  • Sound natural, not memorised
10%

Professionalism

  • Arrive on time
  • Dress appropriately
  • Use polite language
  • Treat it like a real interview
25%

Language

  • Use course vocabulary and grammar
  • Keep talking clearly
  • Correct small mistakes calmly
  • Manage hesitation
25%

Communication

  • Use a clear voice and pauses
  • Maintain eye contact and posture
  • Use natural gestures
  • Recover confidence without notes
